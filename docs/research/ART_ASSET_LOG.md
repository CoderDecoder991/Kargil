# Original texture asset log

The two terrain albedo references below were made with the built-in ImageGen tool on 23 October 2026 and copied into this project. They are invented material art for the hand-authored game map, not photographs of Bajrang or evidence of the 1999 terrain. The source images remain in the tool's generated-image area; these workspace copies are the assets the game loads.

The source textures need a future seam/scale review; shader mixing reduces visible repetition in this prototype. All audio WAV files are generated by the project's `tools/make_audio.py` and remain placeholder effects.

On 23 October 2026, `assets/textures/field_uniform_weave_v1.png` was generated for the first olive-drab clothing pass. It is invented material art, not evidence of the soldiers' documented uniform. Both textures are invented material art, not historical evidence. This procedural model is still an interim asset; a high-quality authored/rigged model and sourced kit references remain open.

## Rigged patrol soldier — 23 October 2026

`assets/models/soldier_tac_danlyvostok_ccby.glb` replaces the procedural patrol body. It is adapted from [“Military tactical suit (LowPolyGameReady)” by DanlyVostok / 1799danly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/military-tactical-suit-lowpolygameready-ef698ce36b1545a78ce592dd3db4c7ed), CC BY 4.0, distributed in the [cktang88/3d-fps character asset set](https://github.com/cktang88/3d-fps). The game hides the source headset mesh, which floats above the head after import, and the prominent modern armor/pad meshes; it adds basic skeletal gait and crouch poses in `scripts/actor.gd`. The model and textures provide a clothed human shape from all camera angles. The model is contemporary tactical art; its remaining vest, cap, pouches and facial features are **generic reconstructed game art, not verified 1999 4 JAT equipment or a likeness of any named patrol member**. Some modern details remain visible and require a later period-specific art pass. The source model did not provide locomotion clips, so the current skeletal motion is simple and needs an authored walk/climb set.

`assets/models/soldier_bamen_candidate.glb` was added on 23 October 2026 for A05l retarget review under the CC BY 4.0 credit recorded in `THIRD_PARTY.md`. It is a generic contemporary armored soldier candidate, not a reconstruction of Lt. Saurabh Kalia, the five soldiers of 4 JAT, or a verified 1999 uniform. The technical validator reports one mesh, one skeleton, 78 bones and humanoid scale. The candidate-only shoulder pose and rifle transform pass the runtime two-hand contact threshold, with measured hand-to-contact distances of about 0.082 m left and 0.090 m right. The file remains opt-in and is not the packaged default until the visual and period-informed review gates pass.

## Reconstructed INSAS visual — 23 October 2026

`assets/models/insas_reconstruction.glb` is retained as a raw copy of the project's own earlier reconstruction from the intact FPS archive. The current optimizer instantiates the adapted `scripts/insas_rifle.gd` directly and bakes it into the 9-surface `assets/models/insas_optimized.tscn`; the v3 pass uses a tapered vented handguard, carry-handle bridge, bolt cover, layered front sight and muzzle, tapered magazine, muted brown furniture and separate polymer/steel surfaces. `scripts/actor.gd` places the rifle across each patrol member's ready-position hands and bakes shoulder/elbow rotations so the trigger hand and support hand meet the weapon. The result is a reconstructed game asset, not a scan or a verified exact 1999 variant. The [mission dossier](Kargil_Mission_1_Bajrang_Patrol_Research_and_Plan.md) records why INSAS is period-possible, but **does not establish that any member of Lt. Kalia's patrol carried one**. The FPS archive remains unchanged.

This keeps the authored hand contact points while giving the silhouette a longer service-rifle proportion. `builds/art_review/patrol_insas_slim_v4.png` is the internal close capture. The packaged input check still reports six rifles and six grips.

The v5 sight pass reduces the rear aperture, sight guards, front sight ears and front sight block so the iron sights do not dominate the isometric silhouette. The packaged input check still reports six rifles and six grips.

The v6 pass reduces the complete fixed-sight bridge and rear sight rails again, including the receiver-top bridge that read as a large black block in the player's screenshot. The front post and sight base were reduced with it. The packaged input check still reports six rifles and six grips.
