## Reconstructed content

The hand-sculpted 96 m test map, route, topographic obstacles, weather sequence, snow cover, figures, clothing, visible AK-pattern rifles, patrol movement, named UI roles, observation point, enemy positions and sight patterns, contact duration and any visible action are **game constructions**. Their precise historical counterparts are not established. The G-triggered reload motion, brace to dramatized incoming fire, ledge-traverse gait and upright reflective aftermath pose are generic animation/gameplay choices; they do not assert a historical reload, confirmed hit, individual injury or personal action by the patrol. The individual soldiers are given no invented private dialogue or special powers. No final radio report, exact enemy count, kill tally, captivity, or alternate historical ending is depicted.

Before any public release, obtain authorized archival and visual references, verify roster spelling and timeline, and seek qualified historical and sensitivity review. The full dossier lists the specific open research questions.
