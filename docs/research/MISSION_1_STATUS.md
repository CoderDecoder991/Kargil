# Mission 1 detailed status

**Project:** *Heights of 1999* — Bajrang patrol mission  
**Last reviewed:** 24 October 2026
**Overall state:** Playable Windows prototype from briefing to memorial with initial original field art; **Mission 1 is not complete**.
**Playable build:** From the source folder, open `bbuilds/prototype-0.1-windows/Play-Mission1.cmd`. From the portable build folder, open `Play-Mission1.cmd`. Double-click on Windows, keep the build folder together and writable, and allow the first launch to import assets. The source `README.md` and portable `BUILD_README.txt` list controls.

## How this file is maintained

- `[x]` means the *specific row's* completion condition is met and supported by evidence. It does not mean its parent workstream or the mission is finished.
- `[ ]` means the condition is still open. The evidence column explains what exists, what remains, or which review is required.
- When a topic is finished, change its leading box from `[ ]` to `[x]`, add the completion date and verification in the evidence column, and update the corresponding A–Z workstream in [`Kargil_FPS_Research_and_A-Z_Build_Log.md`](Kargil_FPS_Research_and_A-Z_Build_Log.md). Keep a broad workstream open while any of its required subtopics remain unchecked. Add new work as a separate row rather than hiding it inside an already checked item.

Reduced the right kneeling thigh rotation from 0.22 to 0.08 radians in `scripts/actor.gd` to lower that knee slightly. Left support leg and ankle tuning are unchanged. Windows PCK exported successfully and packaged crouch/front-side captures rendered at `bbuilds/a05l_candidate_crouch.png` and `bbuilds/a05l_candidate_side.png`. ### Graphics pass — 23 October 2026

Added a procedural mountain sky with a controlled horizon gradient, slightly stronger filmic contrast and saturation, warmer directional sunlight, and terrain grain variation with snow/rock roughness separation. Source and packaged input checks pass. The GT 730 Performance preset measured 20.9 FPS average, 15.9 FPS fifth percentile, and 10.5 FPS minimum in the benchmark scene; this remains a quality pass and does not claim the 30 FPS target on Performance.

### Terrain detail pass — 23 October 2026

Added slope and altitude channels to the terrain vertices so the shader can shade exposed faces separately from shelves. Snow now breaks back on steep faces, rock darkens on exposed slopes, and high snow fields receive a cooler highlight. The packaged terrain capture is `bbuilds/terrain_benchmark.png`; terrain benchmark measured 16.9 FPS average, 10.3 FPS fifth percentile, and 8.4 FPS minimum on the GT 730 at 1280×720.

The follow-up detail layer adds visible irregular strata, narrow fissures, pebble clusters, wind streaks, and a small second-scale scree displacement in `height_at()`. The final fissure contrast is deliberately restrained after visual review so the surface reads as fractured mountain rock rather than a repeating pattern.

### Heavy snow texture pass — 23 October 2026

Widened the authored upper snow bands and added a second snow-texture scale to the terrain shader. Higher shelves now receive granular wind-packed snow flecks over rock, with slope exposure reducing accumulation. This keeps Bajrang's mountain approach visibly snowbound while retaining readable rock on steep faces.

Packaged input validation passes. The latest terrain benchmark measured 14.4 FPS average, 8.7 FPS fifth percentile, and 7.9 FPS minimum on the GT 730 at 1280×720; the added snow texture sample is a measurable cost and the 30 FPS target remains open.

### Rifle material pass — 23 October 2026

Updated the runtime INSAS scene and generator with per-pixel shading, physically separated metallic steel values for the receiver, sight, barrel, and worn edges, and higher-contrast directional wood grain for the stock and handguard. The optimized scene is the asset instantiated by the patrol actor; packaged input validation and the close crouch capture pass after rebuilding the PCK.
